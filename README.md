# CodSoft Data Analytics Internship: Task 3, Data Visualization Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
This project is part of the **CodSoft Data Analytics Internship**.
The goal of Task 3 was to create clear, well-labeled visualizations that present insights from the dataset in an easy-to-understand way.

## 📂 Dataset
**IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset** (from Kaggle), cleaned in Task 1 and explored in Task 2.
- 1,470 employee records
- 32 columns after cleaning

## 🛠️ Tools & Libraries
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib and Seaborn
- Google Colab

## 📊 Visualizations
1. **Bar chart:** Attrition count by department
2. **Line chart:** Average monthly income by job level
3. **Pie chart:** Overall attrition split (left vs stayed)
4. **Histogram:** Age distribution of employees
5. **Scatter plot:** Monthly income vs years at company, colored by attrition

Each chart includes a short written insight explaining what it shows.

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Sales and Human Resources show the highest proportion of attrition relative to their size.
- Income rises sharply at higher job levels, showing a clear reward structure for seniority.
- 16.1% of employees left the company, while 83.9% stayed.
- Most employees are between 28 and 38 years old.
- Employees who left are concentrated in the lower income, lower tenure region, suggesting newer, lower-paid employees are more likely to leave.

## ✅ Outcome
This dashboard-style notebook presents the HR attrition data visually, making the key patterns around department, income, age and tenure easy to understand at a glance.

## 📁 Files in this Repository
- `CodSoft_Task3_DataViz.ipynb`: full notebook with code, charts and insight captions
- `cleaned_HR_Employee_Attrition.csv`: cleaned dataset used for the visualizations
